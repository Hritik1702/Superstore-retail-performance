Superstore Retail Performance Analysis 
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing retail sales performance across the United States, built using the Global Superstore dataset.
Overview
This project explores sales, profit, and operational metrics across 4 regions and multiple product categories to uncover trends and support data-driven business decisions.
Key Features

KPI Cards — Total Sales, Quantity, Profit, Avg Delivery Days, Returns, Total Cities
Regional Filter — Dynamic slicer to drill down by Central, East, South, West
Sales & Profit by Category/Segment — Donut charts breaking down revenue share
Top 10 States — Highest profit and highest sales ranked by state
YoY Trend Analysis — Monthly Sales and Profit comparison across 2019 vs 2020
Sub-Category & Ship Mode Analysis — Bar charts identifying top products and delivery preferences

Tools Used

Power BI Desktop
DAX (measures and KPIs)
Dataset: Global Superstore (Kaggle)

Insights

Consumer segment drives 57% of total sales
Technology is the highest revenue category at ~40%
Tennessee and Florida show negative profit despite reasonable sales — indicating discount or cost issues
Standard Class is the most used shipping mode
